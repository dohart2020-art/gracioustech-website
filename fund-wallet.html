How to fund your wallet

1. Choose an available payment account.
2. Copy the account number.
3. Open your bank app or visit your bank.
4. Transfer the amount you want to add to your wallet.
5. Wait for the payment to be confirmed.
6. Your Gracioustec wallet will be credited automatically.
